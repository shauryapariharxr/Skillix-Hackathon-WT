Privacy Policy

Skillix respects your privacy. This page explains, in general terms, what information we collect when you use our platform and how it is used.

  • We collect account details you provide, such as your name, email address and phone number, to create and manage your account.
  • We collect course progress and activity data to personalise your learning experience.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

Terms & Conditions

By creating an account or enrolling in a course on Skillix, you agree to the following terms:

  • Course content is provided for personal learning use and may not be redistributed or resold.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.
  • Skillix may update course content and pricing from time to time.
  • Accounts found to be in violation of these terms may be suspended.

Refund Policy

We want you to be confident in every course you purchase.

  • Refund requests made within 7 days of enrollment, with less than 20% of the course completed, are eligible for a full refund.
  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Certificates already issued are void upon refund.
